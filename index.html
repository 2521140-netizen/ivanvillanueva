Dr. José Rizal

Born in Calamba, Laguna, Dr. José Rizal was a true polymath: eye surgeon, novelist, sculptor, linguist, and educator. Through his groundbreaking novels, Noli Me Tángere and El Filibusterismo, he exposed the abuses of Spanish colonial rule, igniting a national awakening across the Philippines.
